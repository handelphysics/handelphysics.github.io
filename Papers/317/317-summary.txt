Derives the first analytical formula for the density of dark matter at all length scales from Einstein’s equations and their Newtonian approximation. Dark matter is identified as the energy of the coherent gravitational field of the universe, a field largely its own source, which is drawn into galaxies and clusters in proportion to their own gravitational field via a universal formula whose constant is fixed by the Tully–Fisher relations. The result reproduces galactic rotation curves and the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation in agreement with observations.
